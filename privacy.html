Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

WeFindIt (“we”, “us”) provides a travel search and deal-alert service at wefindit.netlify.app.

What we collect

  • Account data — email address and a hashed password if you create an account.
  • Search data — origin, destination, dates, and passenger count for searches you run.
  • Watched routes — routes you choose to monitor for price drops.
  • Email leads — email addresses submitted via the “Get deal alerts” form.
  • Usage data — approximate request counts for rate limiting and basic analytics.

How we use it

  • To run searches and show results.
  • To send price-drop emails for routes you watch.
  • To improve the service and prevent abuse.

Third parties

  • Duffel — flight (and hotel) search data. See Duffel’s privacy policy.
  • Resend — email delivery for alerts.
  • Netlify — hosting and serverless functions.
We do not sell your data.

Retention

Account data and watched routes are kept until you delete your account or remove the routes. Lead emails are kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of your data by emailing the address associated with your account or the contact below.

Contact

Questions: use the email you signed up with, or reply to any alert email.
