# WeFindIt Soft Launch

## Success criteria (first 14 days)
- 20+ signups
- 50+ searches completed
- 10+ watched routes saved
- At least 1 price-drop email sent successfully
- Average search response time under 5s
- Zero critical errors in Netlify logs

## Timeline
- Day 0: Invite 10–15 people (friends, travel-interested contacts)
- Day 1–3: Monitor signups and searches
- Day 7: Review watched routes and email delivery
- Day 14: Decide whether to expand or iterate

## Invite template

Subject: Try WeFindIt — AI travel deals

Hi,

I’m testing WeFindIt, an AI travel deals tool that searches real flights (and hotels soon) and ranks them by a score that balances price, convenience, and preferences.

You can try it here: https://wefindit.netlify.app

1. Sign up with your email
2. Search a route (type a city name or use an airport code)
3. Watch a route if you want price-drop emails

Would love your feedback on whether the results feel useful.

Thanks,
Liam

## What to track
- Signups (from accounts)
- Searches (Netlify function logs)
- Watched routes (Netlify Blobs)
- Emails sent (Resend dashboard)
- User feedback (reply to invite)

## Known limitations
- Hotels are demo data until Duffel Stays is enabled
- Price alerts run daily (not real-time)
- No booking yet — search and alert only
